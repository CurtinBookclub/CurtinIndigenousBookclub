Session 01

Too Much Lip

Melissa Lucashenko

Wise cracking Kerry Salter rides back into her hometown on a stolen Harley to say goodbye to her dying Pop and ends up fighting to protect their Bundjalung country. Gritty, funny and unflinching.

💡 No pressure to read the whole book. Everything below is a valid way to engage, pick what works for you and come ready to yarn.
