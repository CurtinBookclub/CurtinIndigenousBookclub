About

What is the Indigenous Book Club?

A bi-monthly gathering with the purpose to slow down, connect, and explore stories together. Sessions run for 60–90 minutes, in-person at the library.

There's no pressure to read the whole book. You can engage through a podcast, an audiobook, a short film, or just come ready to listen and chat.

✦ This space is designed for Indigenous staff and students.
